What does this error mean, and what should I do?

The renewal failed because your bank rejected the card check. R-402 is Fieldnotes' code for a failed verification, not for a lack of funds.

  1. Check that the expiry 09/26 and billing address match your bank's records.
  2. Click Update payment method and enter the card again.
  3. If it fails again, your bank may be blocking the charge. Approve it in your bank's app.

Is this expiry date the problem?

Probably not. 09/26 is a valid future date, and an expired card would show its own error.

R-402 usually means the bank rejected the check itself: a billing address mismatch, a wrong CVC, or a block on online payments. Re-entering the card with the exact address on your statement fixes it most often.
